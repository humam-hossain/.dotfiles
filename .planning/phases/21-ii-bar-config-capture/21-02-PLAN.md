## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-21-05 | Denial of Service | `dotfiles-capture.service` | medium | mitigate | Service scheduled with `Nice=19` and `TimeoutStartSec=30s` to prevent background capture from starving desktop UI processes (per D-10) |
| T-21-06 | Denial of Service | `dotfiles-capture.timer` | low | accept | 15-minute interval with `Persistent=true` ensures periodic sync without excessive CPU wakeups; fast-path `cmp -s` skips unchanged files in milliseconds (per D-02, D-08) |
| T-21-07 | Information Disclosure | `notify-send` in `run_capture` | low | mitigate | Notifications use generic text ("Captured updates to repository"), low urgency (`-u low`), and `|| true` so desktop notification failures never break capture (per D-12, D-13) |
| T-21-SC | Tampering | Systemd unit files | low | accept | Units versioned under `stow/systemd/` and stowed to `~/.config/systemd/user/` with restricted permissions |
