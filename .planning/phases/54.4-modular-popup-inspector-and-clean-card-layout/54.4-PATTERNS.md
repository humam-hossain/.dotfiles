**Source:** `W/WeatherGraph.qml:1349-1376`. Apply to all three graph kinds: repaint only on timeline change, popup open, resize; never on hover.

**Source:** `W/WeatherPopup.qml:33` and `StyledPopup.qml:88` (`root.QsWindow?.screen?.width ?? 1920`). Apply to all size bindings.

### Historical-unavailable guard
**Source:** `services/Weather.qml:401-441` slot shape. Every scale/tier/ribbon/draw helper in GM skips `isHistoricalUnavailable`.
