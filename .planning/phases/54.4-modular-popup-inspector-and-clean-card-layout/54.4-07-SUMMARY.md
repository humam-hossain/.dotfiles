# Phase 54.4 Plan 07: Graph 3, Air, UV and Pressure Summary

**The right card now draws the UV index as a WHO tier-coloured spline, a thin EPA tier ribbon along the bottom, amber solar bars with their peak W/m², and a dashed grey pressure line in atm with high and low labels. It has the UV, Solar, AQI, Pressure legend and a five-line tooltip ending with the pressure trend.**

## Accomplishments
- The UV index is drawn on its own 0..max scale, floored at 1. A tierStops gradient gives it hard WHO edges at 3, 6, 8 and 11. The 11+ violet is `#ce93d8` (6.02:1), not the EPA violet (A4).
- The EPA ribbon is a 6px strip at the bottom of the plot. Each available hour with an integer index 1-6 gets one segment in the locked D-54.4-28 colour at full opacity. Index 0, 7, fractional, null and unavailable slots get no segment and read "AQI --".
- The solar bars are amber at 0.45. They stand on the ribbon top and are capped at 40% of the height above it. The peak value, such as "855 W/m²", is written above the tallest bar. PM2.5, PM10, diffRad and DEFRA are not plotted.
- Pressure is a dashed `#b0bec5` line at 0.8 on a scale fitted to the window's exact min and max. A flat window draws at the vertical centre. A 0 pressure breaks the line and never enters the scale. The high and low points are labelled in atm with three decimals.
- The tooltip has five lines: header, `UV 4 Moderate · 489 W/m²`, `AQI Moderate · PM2.5 34 · PM10 41`, `CO 210 · NO₂ 12 · O₃ 88 · SO₂ 4` and `0.995 atm ↘ Falling`.
- The trend compares an hour with the hour 3 hours earlier, in integer hPa (PA-2). When that hour is missing, it uses the nearest earlier real reading (PA-14).

## Decisions Made
- The Graph 3 lines, bars, markers and labels use a sub-geometry whose bottom is the ribbon top. The ribbon keeps the bottom 6px, and no series is drawn over it.
- The solar peak uses the plan 06 `"bar-top"` anchor, so the value is only ever written above its bar.
- PA-14 tie rule: when two earlier readings are equally near i-3, the earlier one is the reference, which keeps the lookback at 3 hours or more.
- Tooltip line 2 takes the tier word from the rounded UV index, so "UV 3" never reads "Low". WWO sends integer indices, so this only matters for a fractional fallback.
- Line 5 is always present on an available slot. A missing pressure reads `-- atm` with no trend, as the plan specifies.
- Graph 3 legend entries have no colour dot. D-54.4-32 names none, and Graph 2's dots come from D-54.4-25.

## For User Confirmation at verify-work
- **PA-2:** the trend is computed in integer hPa. A change below 1 hPa (no change) is Steady, and any other change is Rising or Falling. This uses `PRESSURE_STEADY_HPA = 1`.
- **PA-3:** EPA `#ab47bc` (Very Unhealthy) is 2.99:1 on `#292a2a`. `#c2185b` (Hazardous) is 2.78:1 on `#1f2020` and 2.45:1 on `#292a2a`. Both are kept as locked, and the harness reports them as FINDINGs. Lighter alternatives are `#ce93d8` and `#ec407a`. The EPA index at this location is 2-4 today.
- **PA-14:** the reference is slot i-3 when it holds a real reading. Otherwise it is the nearest earlier real reading, and the earlier one on a tie. With no earlier reading, no trend word is shown.
- **POPUP-04 edge row:** the EPA ribbon and tooltip line 3 are the interim delivery while the AQI card is hidden.
