# Phase 54.4 Plan 06: Graph 2, Rain, Wind and Visibility Summary

**The middle card now draws rain % as a hard-edged blue tier spline, wind/gust/visibility as white solid, white dashed and tier-coloured dotted splines on one shared scale, and teal mm bars with their values above. It has a five-entry legend and the four-line D-54.4-26/36 tooltip.**

## Accomplishments
- Rain probability is drawn on its own 0-100 scale. A vertical gradient built by the new `tierStops` gives it hard tier edges at 30% and 60%. Each edge emits two stops EPS apart, clamped to [0,1] and kept non-decreasing (D-54.4-21..23, Pitfalls 1 and 2).
- Wind, gust and visibility share one invisible 0..max scale, floored at 1. Wind is solid white at 0.9 and gust is dashed white. Visibility is dotted, coloured by its tier: Good, Moderate, Poor and Fog.
- Rain mm bars are teal at 0.6 and appear only for hours with precipitation. They are capped at 40% of the plot height and drawn under the lines. Each value is written above its bar (D-54.4-24). When neighbouring values collide, the larger one is kept.
- The legend reads Rain%, mm, Wind, Gust, Vis, each with a colour dot and a line-style swatch (D-54.4-25, PA-15).
- The tooltip has four lines. L1 always ends with cloud cover. L2 adds Thunder and then Snow, each only when above 0 (D-54.4-36). L3 shows wind, gust and direction. L4 shows visibility in km with its tier word, which delivers the POPUP-03 interim visibility readout.
- Historical-unavailable slots get no bar, spline segment, marker or peak label. Their zero visibility never reads as Fog (T-54.4-20).

## Decisions Made
- mm values use a new `"bar-top"` anchor that offers only the above-centre spot. The fourth fallback of the `"above"` anchor is below-centre, which would put the value over its bar. With a single spot, PA-11's "the smaller one is dropped" holds literally. One cost: a bar in the last column can lose its value off the right edge. The value still shows in tooltip L2.
- `tierStops` sorts edges by offset and colours each pair from the vTop side. Inverted and flat ranges therefore stay valid, and offsets stay non-decreasing when two edges lie within EPS.
- Wind, gust and visibility peaks are skipped when the series maximum is 0 or less, the same rule as rain.
- Tooltip lines 2-4 drop a segment whose value is not a finite number, instead of printing a made-up zero.
