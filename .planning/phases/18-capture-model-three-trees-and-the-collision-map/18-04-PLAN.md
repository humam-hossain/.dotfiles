<objective>
Reconcile the redistribution table against the generated collision map, archive the two files that
have no capture future, and move the two non-hypr files into `restow/` with their content decision
resolved.

D-26 is the ordering constraint this plan sits inside: the map is generated and committed first
(plan 18-01), and only then does anything move. Every package boundary must be a mechanical
consequence of the map. Intuition gets `hypridle.conf` and `hyprlock.conf` wrong — they look like
quiet personal config, and `install_file__auto_backup` renames the link away on firstrun, which puts
them in `restow/`.

RESEARCH F-2 adds a step the CONTEXT's sequence does not carry and which is the phase's most likely
execution-time failure: all twelve live counterparts of repo-root `.config/` are plain files, never
symlinks. GNU Stow 2.4.1 exits 1 rather than overwrite a non-link, and every `arch/*.sh` call site
runs under `set -euo pipefail`. Every migration task therefore moves the live real file aside before
stowing, and keeps the aside copy until `test -L` confirms the link.

Purpose: place every file the repo-root tree held, with a recorded reason, before the tree is removed.
Output: the redistribution table in `docs/`, two archived files, and two `restow/` packages linked live.
</objective>
