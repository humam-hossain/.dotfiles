<objective>
Turn `verify` and `capture` into real wrapper-owned subcommands with real behaviour.

Today both exit at `main`'s allowlist check. Adding them to `ALLOWLIST` without the matching dispatch
arms would be worse than leaving them out: the catch-all routes them into `run_install_family`, which
calls `preflight` and then execs upstream's setup script with a subcommand upstream does not define.
D-61 and D-48 are therefore a matched pair and land together.

`run_verify` exists to catch the drift a content comparison cannot see. Both destroying installer
primitives leave the repo file untouched, so a content-only check reports "no drift" in exactly the
case that matters. Link-ness is asserted first, always.

`run_capture` is the only thing in this repo that writes into the working tree from the live
filesystem. Its refusals are its point: a dirty mirror, an untracked mirror, an absent mirror, a live
symlink into the repo, a live counterpart that has vanished. The dirty check as the CONTEXT spells it
cannot detect two of those — `git diff --quiet HEAD` exits 0 for untracked *and* for nonexistent
paths, which is precisely the case with the most uncommitted work at stake and no way to recover it.
The corrected two-part test lands here.

Purpose: make the two registered subcommands real, and make their refusals correct before anything
depends on them.
Output: `run_verify()`, `run_capture()`, the `ALLOWLIST` entries, the two dispatch arms, and the
assert section proving the dispatch.
</objective>
