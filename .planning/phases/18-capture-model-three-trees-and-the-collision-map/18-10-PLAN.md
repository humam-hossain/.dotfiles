<objective>
Populate the generated half of `restow/README.md`, then close CAP-03's remaining demonstration: an
assert that exits non-zero when a path's tree placement contradicts the map.

The tag table is the last piece of ROADMAP criterion 1. Every `restow/` package must be tagged with
its recovery class, and the tag must come from the same source as the tree column — the map's two
outcome columns — so that a new primitive needs no second lookup table and a tag correction cannot
leave a stale copy behind. That is why the table is generated rather than written, and why its region
is regenerated and diffed exactly the way the map itself is.

The mis-filed fixture is the demonstration the ROADMAP asks for by name. It has to live inside the
real tree, because that is what "tree placement" means — which makes its cleanup a `trap`, not a
trailing line. A permanently committed mis-filed fixture was rejected: it would need an exclusion list
to stop `verify` flagging it, and an exception mechanism is precisely what this phase avoids everywhere
else.

Purpose: finish the tagging half of CAP-01 and the placement half of CAP-03, then run the phase gate.
Output: the populated table, the generator's table mode, the mis-filed-fixture check, and a green suite.
</objective>
