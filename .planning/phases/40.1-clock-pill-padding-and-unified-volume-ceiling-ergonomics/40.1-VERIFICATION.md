# Phase 40.1 Verification: Clock Pill Padding and Unified Volume Ceiling Ergonomics

## Goal Achievement

The Phase 40.1 goal has been completely achieved:
- `ClockWidget.qml` status bar clock pill enforces 10px horizontal breathing room via 5px left/right margins on `RowLayout` plus `BarGroup` container padding (5px), perfectly matching the 10px left sidebar button and ~9px Resources pill (CLOCK-01, D-01).
- `ClockWidget.qml` retains the 8px non-glyph spacer (`implicitWidth: 8`), second precision (`DateTime.time`), and full date formatting (`DateTime.longDate`) (D-02).
- `capture/ii/.config/illogical-impulse/config.json` and live `~/.config/illogical-impulse/config.json` define `"volumeCeiling": 1.5` under `"audio"` as the desktop-wide single source of truth (VOL-01, D-03).
- `stow/hypr/.config/hypr/custom/keybinds.lua` unbinds upstream `XF86AudioRaiseVolume`, parses `config.json` in pure Lua at compositor startup/reload with safe fallback, and binds `wpctl set-volume @DEFAULT_AUDIO_SINK@ 2%+ -l <ceiling>` with zero subprocess or subshell overhead during key repeats (VOL-01, D-04, T-40.1-02).
- `Config.qml` declares `property real volumeCeiling: 1.5` on `JsonObject audio`, enabling Quickshell's C++ `JsonAdapter` to reactively expose the setting (VOL-01, D-05).
- `Audio.qml` exposes `readonly property real maxVolume: Config.options?.audio?.volumeCeiling ?? 1.5` as the central ceiling contract for all Quickshell UI controls and services (VOL-01, D-05).
- `QuickSliders.qml` binds the volume slider's upper bound `to: Audio.maxVolume` (0.0 to 1.5), provides a tactile stop notch at 100% volume via `stopIndicatorValues: [1.0]` without double normalization, and formats `tooltipContent: \`${Math.round(value * 100)}%\`` for accurate percentage readouts across the entire 0% to 150% range (VOL-02, D-06, D-07, D-08).
- `Audio.qml` mouse scroll volume controls (`incrementVolume()`) dynamically clamp to `root.maxVolume` (`Math.min(root.maxVolume, Audio.sink.audio.volume + step)`), automatically unmute audio on increment (`Audio.sink.audio.muted = false`), and enforce 2% step size (1% below 10%) matching Hyprland keybind granularity (VOL-02, D-09, D-10, D-11).
- All QML modifications are deployed as leaf symlinks via `restow/quickshell/` without modifying `vendor/dots-hyprland`.
- The 5-section automated test harness (`scripts/phase40.1-clock-volume-assert.sh`) and `./arch/dots-hyprland.sh verify --strict` pass with `FAIL=0 FINDINGS=0`.
