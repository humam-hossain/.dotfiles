## 9. Architecture Review & Simplification

User requested comprehensive review across all decisions to eliminate conflicts and simplify the implementation. Three simplifications were approved:
1. Reconciled click vs hover model: Replaced button-style pointing hand cursor with `Qt.ArrowCursor` and removed persistent toggle state in favor of pure hover highlight.
2. Eliminated double-container nesting: Retained `BarGroup` as the outer surface/animation provider; `WeatherBar.qml` focuses strictly on inner content and hover highlight.
3. Coordinated animations: Allowed `BarGroup` to natively absorb `Revealer` layout expansion without competing duration drivers.
