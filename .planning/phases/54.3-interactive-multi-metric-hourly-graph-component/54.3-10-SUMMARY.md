- **Found during:** Task 3 (first full harness run: 1 FAIL on a gate that passes manually)
- **Issue:** `sed -n '/a/,/b/p' | grep -q pattern` under `set -o pipefail`: grep -q exits on first match while sed keeps writing, sed dies with SIGPIPE (141), and the pipeline reports failure. - **Fix:** All four new `sed | grep -q` gates rewritten to the harness's own counting idiom (`grep -c ... || true` + `-ge 1` test), which drains the pipe and is deterministic.
