<threat_model>
ASVS L1 Threat Model & Mitigations:
- **T-50-07: Malformed or Tampered Telemetry Ingestion**
  - Threat: Reading unvalidated or corrupt JSON data from `benchmark-latest.json` could cause script crashes, division-by-zero errors in `awk`, or false-positive assertion passes.
  - ASVS L1 Mapping: V5.1.4 Data Validation & Parsing.
  - Mitigation: `phase50-opt-assert.sh` validates JSON syntax via `jq empty` before extracting stage metrics, and applies strict numeric bounds validation.
- **T-50-08: Benchmarking Session Interference via Background Processes**
  - Threat: Competing video players or web browsers running during profiling could spike CPU/GPU loads and invalidate baseline comparisons.
  - ASVS L1 Mapping: V11.1 Business Logic & Measurement Integrity.
  - Mitigation: `scripts/profile-quickshell.sh` pauses active MPRIS players via `playerctl pause -a` before taking baseline measurements and enforces settling delays.
- **T-50-09: Repository Contamination or Submodule Drift**
  - Threat: Artifact generation writing outside tracked planning folders or modifying submodules during test runs.
  - ASVS L1 Mapping: V1.1.2 Secure Supply Chain & Version Control Integrity.
  - Mitigation: Section 5 of `phase50-opt-assert.sh` runs `./arch/dots-hyprland.sh verify --strict` and verifies `git status --porcelain vendor/dots-hyprland` is empty.
</threat_model>
