Context from Debug Investigation (.planning/debug/media-artist-visibility.md):
1. In Plan 48-01, `Media.qml` formatted track title and artist into a single HTML string: `<span style="color: colOnLayer1">${title}</span><span style="color: colSubtext"> • ${artist}</span>` inside a single `StyledText` with `elide: Text.ElideRight`.
2. When the track title is long, `Text.ElideRight` elides characters from the right side of the combined string. Because the artist was appended at the end, the artist was chopped off first, disappearing completely on long songs/videos (e.g. YouTube "How to Solder TINY SMD Components...").
3. The user requested:
   - The artist should always be visible.
   - The title should be shortened/elided first when reaching maximum width.
   - For narrow monitors (e.g. <= 1200px or `useShortenedForm > 0`), an adaptive threshold should handle the narrow screen cleanly (falling back to compact mode / title only).
4. By splitting title and artist into two elements in `rowLayout`:
   - `mediaTitleText` gets `Layout.fillWidth: true` and `elide: Text.ElideRight`.
   - `mediaArtistText` gets `Layout.fillWidth: false`, `color: Appearance.colors.colSubtext`, and `visible: Config.options.bar.verbose && (root.useShortenedForm === 0) && Boolean(activePlayer?.trackArtist)`.
   - In Qt Quick `RowLayout`, non-filling items receive their intrinsic width first. Therefore, `mediaArtistText` is always fully displayed, while `mediaTitleText` takes the remaining space and elides with `...`.
</context>
