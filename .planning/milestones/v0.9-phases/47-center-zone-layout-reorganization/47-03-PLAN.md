Context from Debug Investigation (.planning/debug/sidebar-toggle.md):
1. In upstream dots-hyprland, `ClockWidget` was hosted in `rightCenterGroup` (located to the right of Workspaces adjacent to `barRightSideMouseArea`), where toggling `GlobalStates.sidebarRightOpen` made spatial sense.
2. In Phase 47, `ClockWidget` was moved to the left of Workspaces into `leftCenterGroup`. The implementation preserved the `MouseArea` wrapping with `sidebarRightOpen` toggle.
3. In UAT Test 3, the click toggle did not fire because `ClockWidget.qml`'s inner `MouseArea` (`mouseArea`) has `acceptedButtons: Qt.LeftButton` by default and consumed the press event.
4. During UAT, the user confirmed: "nope it does not and it does need to do anything when i click on it" — colloquial English indicating the clock does not need to perform any action on click, and the toggle should simply be removed.
5. In addition to fixing the gap, removing the outer `MouseArea` eliminates redundant nesting (`leftCenterGroup` MouseArea wrapping `leftCenterGroupContent` BarGroup) and turns `leftCenterGroup` into a direct `BarGroup`, mirroring `middleCenterGroup` and `weatherGroup`. `BarGroup` natively computes its `implicitWidth` and `implicitHeight` and includes built-in 250ms `emphasizedDecel` width animations.
</context>
