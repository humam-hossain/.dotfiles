<objective>
Reorganize `restow/quickshell/.config/quickshell/ii/modules/ii/bar/BarContent.qml` into three modular, decoupled, and easily swappable zones:
1. Left Section: Bare `LeftSidebarButton` (screenRounding margin) -> `[ Resources ]` pill -> `[ UtilButtons ]` pill -> trailing flexible spacer.

Enforce Option 1 Dynamic Spacing Defense to prevent component collision, deploy via restow `--no-folding`, reload Quickshell live, and verify through `scripts/phase33-layout-assert.sh` Sections 1-3.

Purpose: Deliver the candidate bar layout fulfilling LAYOUT-01 and LAYOUT-03, providing uncompromised visual geometry ready for live dual-monitor evaluation.
